Design-system pass over the existing portal — same screens, same IA, desktop density kept. Sidebar rows now carry Material Symbols at 44px with primary-container selection; page titles use Headline Small 24/32 at weight 600; filter chips and the Activity/Challenge toggle use the student app's selected-chip treatment (secondary-container + check, outlined when unselected); status becomes Tag pills on the accent scale instead of coloured text; every button is a pill; form fields are 38–40px outlined inputs at 8px radius; cards carry shadow-soft. Terminology and the Challenge/Activity model now match what students see — Employer sponsored, XP, Skills, Difficulty, Duration.
